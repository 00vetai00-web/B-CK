The essentials

Everything a modern player should have.

One small Node server on your PC, Pi, or Android phone routes every stream — so your credentials stay put and your provider only ever sees one IP.

Live TV with real EPG

Automatic now/next, full guide grid from your XMLTV, program search and catch-up.

Movies & Series

Posters, resume, watched marks, next-episode autoplay, skip intro and full-season pickers.

TMDB info & trailers

Cast, rating, runtime and the official trailer, fetched server-side so your key stays yours.

HLS-rewrite privacy

Every manifest URL, segment, key and EXT-X-MAP is rewritten through your proxy. Nothing leaks.

Multi-profile

Separate profiles with per-user resume, favorites and hidden channels. Kids mode included.

Chromecast + PWA

Cast to any TV on the LAN. Install as an app on iPhone, Android, macOS, Windows or Linux.

Built for every screen

Cinematic on your TV. Snappy on your phone.

The same virtualized UI stays smooth on libraries with tens of thousands of items.

Browse like a streaming service.

Automatic category rows, Continue Watching, Top 10, Shuffle — powered by your own classifier that reads URL paths, group titles and SxxExx naming.

  • Live TV, Movies and Series split automatically
  • Continue Watching restored per-profile
  • Cross-type search finds anything in one field
  • "Help me pick" quiz for those long evenings

A player that actually works.

Skip intro, next-episode autoplay, 10-second scrub, playback speed, PiP, keyboard control and a full CC/audio-track picker with online subtitle search.

  • In-player episode picker with season switcher
  • OpenSubtitles + Wyzie search on any title
  • Sleep timer, aspect-ratio and quality controls
  • Cast to TV without leaving the couch

Info + trailers for every title.

Metadata comes live from The Movie Database — fetched server-side so your API key never touches the client, and the same key covers every film and show in your playlist.

  • Rating, cast, genres, runtime, plot
  • YouTube trailer built into the detail page
  • Aggressive server-side caching for instant re-opens
